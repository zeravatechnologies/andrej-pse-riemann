## Addendum (personal edition): your missions

This addendum exists only in the `pse-riemann-andrej` package. The public package starts with no missions.

**1. Continuous transformation between QRH methods.** Is there a continuous function that carries the high-side exponent of one QRH method to the next (Liu → Xu–Sang: E_XS = E_Liu + T, confirmed), and does its extension (t, w, new families) move B toward the goals (0.87; 5/6; 3/4; 1/2) or away from them? State: within the Liu/Xu–Sang family the transformation IS continuous; the extensions approach the walls (~0.870 / 5/6), not 1/2. Every new paper through the door adds a point. The arXiv watch (once a day) runs for this mission.

**2. Higher degree: does 7/8 carry over?** Does the 7/8 method (Liu, Xu–Sang) carry over to L-functions of higher degree (GL(2), Artin), how does the exponent change with the degree d, and is there a T_d between families? Goal 1 (transferability map) is the default; goals 2 (E_d and T_d conditionally) and 3 (tracking only) the app only proposes. The main obstacle: Gauss sums → the cubic theta have no known analogue.

**3. The complex version: does the bound bend?** (a) Does the bound depend on the height γ? Within the method σ*(γ) ≡ B (closed, OURS-UNREVIEWED); outside it, open. (b) What are the exponent functions with a complex argument?

**What else the personal edition carries:** QRH source cards (Liu, Xu–Sang, Langlands literature), four method packs (Liu, Xu–Sang, 11/12, 7/8) for the acceptance test, the research reports (`/usr/share/doc/pse-riemann/research/`), the QRH lab experiments (V(ℓ), exact polynomial B*, transformation Liu→XS, ladder of walls, license iteration, complex version, adversarial mode) and the *Working paper (missions 2 and 3)*.
